# Blinkr — The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched

Entry for [The Worst App Idea Ever Pitched](https://make.fun/c/u-plucky-sparrow-67-the-worst-app-idea-ever-pitched-a0396b23) on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

## What it does

`main.py` writes one sentence (230 characters) to `/output/entry.txt`,
pitching the worst, most useless app imaginable:

> Blinkr is a premium subscription app that uses AI to remind you to blink
> while you sleep, gently vibrating your phone under your pillow every time
> it detects your eyes are closed so you never miss a single unconscious
> blink again.

A problem nobody has — blinking while asleep — solved with total founder
confidence, a premium subscription, and a phone buzzing under your pillow
all night.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the pitch to `/output/entry.txt`. Deterministic: the same pitch on
every run.
